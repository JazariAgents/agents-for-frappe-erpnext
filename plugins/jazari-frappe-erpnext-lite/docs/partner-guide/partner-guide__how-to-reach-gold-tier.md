# How to reach Gold tier

### **Additional Benefits**

- **More trust:** Gold partners are expected to meet a PMM level 5, which is more stringent and demonstrates higher process excellence compared to level 4, which is mandatory for Silver. Getting a PMM level 5 drives more trust and credibility for gold partners.
- **Better listing:** Gold partners are visible right at the top of the [main partner listing page](https://frappe.io/partners/list). 
- **More leads:** With better listing, website mentions and recognition, the Gold Partner profile page receives higher website traffic and more leads.
- **High-value deals:** Gold partners get priority listing and attracts highest-value enterprise prospects.

### **Eligibility Criteria**

- **Monthly recurring revenue of $7500 / INR 6,30,000 or above on Frappe Cloud.** This includes revenue from both directly and indirectly billed accounts. This approximately means ~12 small business customers (with ~$50 sites on Frappe Cloud) or 3-4 mid to large sized customers.
- **PMM Score of 5:** You must initiate a PMM audit at least 60 days prior to your expected upgrade date. Our team of experts will audit your processes. And only after you successfully meet the PMM Score 5 criteria, you will be listed as a Gold partner.



The MRR contribution increases every year. It is recommended that you verify the criteria with the Frappe team.
