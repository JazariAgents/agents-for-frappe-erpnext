PRM portal is the Partner Relationship Management Portal. It is the one place you can view and manage all the details of your partnership. You can find everything here including leads that reach out via partner listing, leads shared by Frappe team, marketing resources, invoices for linked customers, certified members in your organisation, and much more.

### How to access PRM on Frappe Cloud:

To access Partner portal on FC, click on the **Partnership** section on the dashboard side bar and you should see the Overview page and other tabs.



![Partnership section](https://drive.frappe.io/api/method/drive.api.embed.get_file_content?embed_name=9ov6n4dics&parent_entity_name=8vvrjnhdjf)

Partner Lead

You can see all the leads assigned to your team in this tab. In each lead, you can see the lead details and update them. Here are some best practices while reaching out to leads. 

1. **Respond promptly:** Reach out to leads as soon as they are passed to you. 
2. **Maintain status and follow-ups:** Maintain follow-ups and status to inform the deal owner.
3. **Why lead hygiene and conversion matter:** Proper lead management and faster conversions for the leads passed via PRM will encourage sales team to pass more leads to you.

### **Partner Member**

Your Frappe Cloud team members are considered as Partner Members. You can view the team members list from **Settings** > **Team**.

  


![](https://drive.frappe.io/api/method/drive.api.embed.get_file_content?embed_name=7dpu2he15r&parent_entity_name=8vvrjnhdjf)

You add a new Partner member click on **Add Member** and send an Invite to them to join your team. You can also assign a predefined role to the member while inviting the member.



![](https://drive.frappe.io/api/method/writer.api.embed.get?id=2s4h12l3s2)

Note: *As a member of a partner team, if you have your own Frappe Cloud team, please **Switch to Partner Team** to access PRM.*



![](https://drive.frappe.io/api/method/writer.api.embed.get?id=7s7mvlhmlg)

### **Permissions**

To give restricted dashboard access to your team members you can configure roles and permissions, based on which you can control the dashboard access for each members.

To create new roles, navigate to **Settings** > **Permissions**.

  


![](https://drive.frappe.io/api/method/drive.api.embed.get_file_content?embed_name=5hc4qbaum1&parent_entity_name=8vvrjnhdjf)

Once the role is created, click on ‘**+**‘ icon to add members to the new role created.



![](https://drive.frappe.io/api/method/writer.api.embed.get?id=0sks92mqq9)

From the settings tab, you can choose which access to be given to which role. Under ‘**Allow Partner Access**‘, you can configure more granular access in partnership tab.
