Frappe is committed to building world-class open-source software to help businesses of all sizes to operate and grow. We believe the future is open-source. Open-source software has many benefits such as the ability to get started with no costs, no licensing fees, no vendor lock-in, a growing community and innovation. We are on a mission to put an end to rigid, licensed and legacy enterprise software. 



Frappe’s story started as a hobby project while trying to build a [WordPress of ERPs](https://frappe.io/story). Today, Frappe is much more than ERPNext. We’re a technology ecosystem with a developer platform, developer tools, business apps and a hosting platform. 



Open-source is not the only reason Frappe products are growing. Customers love Frappe for being user-friendly, feature-rich and scalable. Frappe has grown over 40% year on year since inception, with customers across all continents and 100+ countries. 



The commitment to building great products led us to discontinue implementation services in 2023. Since then, our primary focus has been to build excellent products and build a partner ecosystem to support the ecosystem's growth.



# Frappe’s growth

If you’re exploring Frappe, a valid question you might have is, “Is Frappe growing?”. Short answer: Yes - and consistently so. Frappe is growing slowly, steadily and organically. An organic growth path was fitting for Frappe because we believe that to win the market, we have to be a truly world-class product. And building a world-class product is a long journey with no shortcuts. 



Taking the organic growth path has been challenging, especially when suboptimal competitors win over the short term, but those are the times we double down on the fundamentals and keep improving the product. 



Organic growth strategy has enabled Frappe’s balanced growth. The demand (customer awareness) and the supply (partner ecosystem) has grown slowly and steadily.

### Why link a customer account to a partner?

Linking a partner code to a customer account helps Frappe to track that the partner is actively servicing the customer. Revenue from such customers are also considered part of the partner contribution, which is included in the total amount considered for the tier upgrades. However, revenue from such accounts does not earn any commissions. To earn commissions, the customer has to select the “paid by partner”, but by doing so, the customer still owns their FC account, and the billing happens via the partner. In such cases, partners do not have any access to the customer account and can see limited information about their monthly invoice value.

### How to link a customer account to a partner?

**Step 1: Partner shares partner code with the customer**

Partners can access their unique code from the PRM portal. From Partnerships > Overview tab. Partners can copy this code and send it to their customers.



[Screenshot on the live page]

**Step 2:  Customer adds partner code to their account**

Customers can find the option to do so from Settings > Profile > Frappe Partner. Customers can update a unique partner code shared by their partner over here. 



[Screenshot on the live page]

**Step 3: Partner accepts the link request**

The Partner receives an email notification and will be able to see the link request on the Frappe Cloud portal. Approving the link request is mandatory for the customer account to be linked under the partner’s account. 



**Step 4: Final approval from Frappe (not mandatory)**

Final approval from Frappe is not required for all cases. But in case an old customer account is being attempted to be linked to the partner, Frappe prefers to approve to verify the details. 

### What changes after the partner code linking is complete

Partners will be able to see the linked customer invoices and plans in the **Partnership > Contributions** section

Entry Level tier is where you start your partnership.



Remember, your benefits get better as you grow into the partnership. Yet Entry Partnership offers a wide range of benefits from the get-go. 

## Benefits for entry level partners

- **Limited recognition:** Get officially recognised as a Frappe Partner and build trust with prospects by showcasing your company on the Partner page.
- **Access to Frappe partner portal:** Get access to a dedicated portal to manage leads, track opportunities, and collaborate seamlessly with the Frappe team.
- **Access to partners.frappe.io:** A discussion forum open to all partners and Frappe team members.
- **Access to Frappe Telegram and WhatsApp groups:** Stay connected with the Frappe community and team through real-time discussions, updates, and support channels.
- **Access to Marketing Resources:** Leverage ready-to-use marketing materials to effectively position and promote Frappe products to your prospects.
- **Access to Partner events (webinars and trainings):** Participate in exclusive webinars and training sessions to continuously build your product knowledge and expertise.
- **10% Discounts:** You get an instant discount on your Frappe Cloud Invoice.

## Common issues and getting help

#### Product Knowledge

As an Entry Partner, you are still building familiarity with the product and may encounter edge cases where you're unsure whether a feature is supported or how to implement it. Certification is a good starting point. But certifications are like getting a driving license, but far from real expertise. 



While we provide limited hands-on support for implementations, you can refer to the following resources to help yourself:

- **Frappe Support Portal (support.frappe.io):** Raise support tickets for product-related queries.
- **Community Forum (discuss.frappe.io):** Learn from existing discussions and seek help from the global community.
- **Partner WhatsApp / Telegram Groups:** Ask questions and get quick inputs from fellow partners and the Frappe team.
- **Co-deliver projects with an existing partner (frappe.io/partners/list):** Collaborate with experienced partners to learn on real projects.
- **Documentation (docs.frappe.io):** Refer official product documentation.
- **Community developers:** For advanced technical requirements on Frappe Framework, reach out to experienced community developers such as Resilient Tech ([info@resilient.tech](mailto:info@resilient.tech)) and Aerele Technologies ([vignesh@aerele.in](mailto:vignesh@aerele.in)).
- **Reach out to Frappe:** When needed, connect with someone from the Frappe team for guidance.

#### **Hiring and Training**

Hiring is a hard problem and there is no magic spell to get it right. Getting your first hire right is one of the most consequential events to kick start your Frappe business. Here are some resources that can help in hiring:

- You can view a list of Frappe School Certified Participants [here](https://frappe.school/lms/certified-participants).
- You can post job openings [here](https://frappe.school/lms/job-openings).

#### **Initial Lead Generation**

As a new partner, your primary focus should be to reach the existing Frappe community and prospects. You won’t receive leads immediately due to limited visibility. Here are some tips to generate leads:

- Introduce yourself as a Frappe partner on LinkedIn
- Add a Frappe logo on your website
- Add SEO keywords for your region (e.g., ERPNext partners in Kenya)
- Add product demos on YouTube: They are a good way to get leads over the long term
- Add success stories on your website and LinkedIn
- Answer queries on discuss.frappe.io, which helps connect directly with prospects
- Make a profile on [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) or equivalent hiring websites in your region
- Implement a contact form and CRM to manage your leads

These efforts ensure that the right prospects can find you easily. 

#### How to Pitch and Convert

A 5-hour recorded Sales [Training for ERPNext](https://school.frappe.io/lms/courses/frappe-sales-bootcamp) is the best resource to learn how to pitch and convert ERPNext customers. It is highly recommended that your sales team watch this course. 



Remember, the first few conversions are a cost of learning and it is absolutely ok if you don’t make a very high profit on them. Your aim should be to break even and gain experience.

We’ve kept the partnership criteria simple and transparent. Here are the steps to become a partner:

1. **Registered Entity:** You need to own an IT services business verifiable via a website and if required, some additional company proofs verifying the registered company address.
2. **Certifications:** You must have at least two full-time team members who are certified in ERPNext or the Frappe Framework. Certifications can be earned through Frappe School. Only complete ERPNext and Frappe Framework certifications count. Single-module or associate-level certificates are not eligible.
3. **Early success:** You should already have Frappe customers amounting to more than $100 per month on Frappe Cloud

Once you’re done with all these, you can accept the partnership agreement and submit your partnership request. Frappe will review and approve your partnership application. Once done, congratulations! You’re listed as an entry-level partner for your region.

# How to reach Bronze tier

### **Additional Benefits**

- **Better listing:** Bronze partners are visible on the [main partner listing page](https://frappe.io/partners/list), right below Silver, with a large card layout. They get a profile page to list their company information, website, address, success stories and strengths.
- **Success stories:** Bronze partners can contribute [success stories](https://frappe.io/stories) and get features as the implementation partner under the story.
- **Website mentions:** Bronze partners get featured on the [ERPNext industry](https://frappe.io/erpnext/industry), Frappe Apps landing pages, and more.
- **Recognition:** Bronze partners receive a shout-out on Frappe’s LinkedIn page, partner blogs, podcasts and more.
- **More leads:** With more features, mentions and recognition, the Bronze Partner profile page receives higher website traffic and more leads.
- **Access to partner council**: Partner council is a monthly online meeting between partners and Frappe. Through this forum, Frappe aims to listen to partner concerns and make key decisions democratically.

### **Eligibility Criteria**

- **Monthly recurring revenue of $750 / INR 63,000 or above on Frappe Cloud.** This includes revenue from both directly and indirectly billed accounts. This approximately means ~12 small business customers (with ~$50 sites on Frappe Cloud) or 3-4 mid to large sized customers.
- **PMM Score of 3 or above:** You must initiate a PMM audit at least 30 days prior to your expected upgrade date. Our team will conduct a structured audit of your processes. And only after you successfully meet the PMM Score 3 criteria, you will be listed as an Emerging partner.

The MRR contribution increases every year. It is recommended that you verify the criteria with the Frappe team.

# How to reach Emerging tier

### **Additional Benefits**

- **Customer trust:** When customers choose partners, they look for someone who can be trusted, who runs a business with systematic processes and someone who keeps their promises. This is exactly what the Emerging tier signifies. To become an Emerging partners, partners have to meet the level 3 mentioned in the **Partner Maturity Program (PMM).** 
- **Better listing:** Emerging partners are visible on the [main partner listing page](https://frappe.io/partners/list), right **below Bronze**, with a **smaller card layout**. Website visitors on the partner listing page can click on the logo and reach out to you directly. Leads created from here get passed to you directly from the PRM portal.

### **Eligibility Criteria**

- **Monthly recurring revenue of $350 (or INR equivalent) or above on Frappe Cloud.** This includes revenue from both directly and indirectly billed accounts.
- **PMM Score of 3 or above:** You must initiate a PMM audit at least 30 days prior to your expected upgrade date. Our team of experts will audit your processes. And only after you successfully meet the PMM Score 3 criteria, you will be listed as an Emerging partner.



The MRR contribution increases every year. It is recommended that you verify the criteria with the Frappe team.

If you’re here, you’re likely evaluating Frappe products for a prospect or have already resold / implemented them for your customers. You like our products and are thinking about building a business vertical with Frappe products. Great! You’ve reached the right place. 



This guide contains everything you need to know about Frappe Partnerships, what are the benefits, rationale, and next steps to help you navigate the journey after becoming a Frappe Partner.



# Frappe’s business model

Frappe’s business model has evolved over the years from a SaaS + services-led business model to hosting. As on today, Frappe Cloud is the primary revenue driver for Frappe.



Frappe Cloud is a specialised hosting platform for Frappe Apps that simplifies hosting for small businesses, large enterprises and partners. It comes with the benefits of multi-tenancy and managed hosting. Frappe Cloud simplifies installs, upgrades, backups, scalability and much more with a user friendly UI that achieves everything with a few clicks. 



Frappe Cloud allows partners to focus on solving core business problems for their customers and leave the DevOps to Frappe. With Frappe Cloud, we not only aim to provide a world-class hosting experience but also offer application-level product warranties to end-customers. Which is why most of the Partners who join Frappe Partnership are already hosting Frappe products on Frappe Cloud. 



Frappe’s business model can be summarised simply as: 



*“We create excellent products and developer tools, all 100% open source. You only pay for hosting on Frappe Cloud, through which Frappe aims to provide a reliable, scalable and secure hosting, and provide product support.”* 



# Role of partners

If you’ve already read about Frappe’s business model, one thing should be clear to the readers. **Frappe does not compete with our partners.** Partners are central to the adoption and success of Frappe products. Partners are the reason for customers to adopt Frappe products. As on today, ~70% of Frappe Cloud revenue is serviced via Partners.



Here is the role Frappe Partners play:

- End-to-end Frappe products implementations
- New feature development and customisations
- L1 Product support
- Annual Maintenance Cost through renewals
- Contributing apps to the Frappe marketplace



Here is the role Frappe plays:

- Build excellent FOSS products
- Frappe Cloud Hosting
- L1 Product warranty
- Trainings through Frappe School



Frappe services complement our partners beautifully. The relationship is balanced and mutually beneficial — Frappe supports the partner’s growth as much as vice versa. 



# Profile of existing Frappe partners

The first type of partner profile we attracted are companies that have experience implementing Frappe products. This is because all our products are open-source and that does not gate-keep anyone from implementing them. Some of our old partners became authorised partners after as high as 30+ ERPNext implementations. 



The second type of partners we attract are software development companies that start off with custom software development for end customers, and eventually stumble upon Frappe products and choose them over building apps from scratch. These partners typically have strong development capabilities. 



The third category of partners we attract are experienced companies that have already built strong businesses with SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, WordPress, Odoo and many more. These partners are strong in functional and consulting capabilities. They like the scalability and ease-of implementations for Frappe products and add Frappe to their portfolio.



# Conclusion

To sum up, Partners are not just an enabler for the growth of the Frappe ecosystem, they’re a driving force. Partners play a key role in helping customers adopt Frappe products. Frappe partnership program enables us to discover, nurture and empower Frappe partners in the journey of mutual growth.

Partners play a key role in not just enabling Frappe's growth, but also driving it. By becoming an authorised Frappe partner, you get the opportunity to ride the growth wave that Frappe is experiencing. 



Here are the key benefits of partnering with Frappe,

- **Official partner listing:** We list you on our partner page and make sure you get all the attention from potential customers.
- **Discounts:** Generous discounts on Frappe Cloud payments for Emerging, Gold, Silver & Bronze partners.
- **Product Support:** Exclusive access to Frappe office hours and support services to resolve product queries within SLAs. 
- **Deal assistance:** Get a perfect closure to your deals with Frappe-backed advantages.
- **Trainings:** Exclusive access to trainings to enable product expertise, process excellence and more.
- **PMM consulting and audits:** Get 1:1 consulting for building quality sales, implementation and support processes. 
- **Recognition:** From LinkedIn shoutouts to YouTube videos, you have opportunities to be featured across various Frappe platforms.
- **Co-marketing:** Co-sponsor events and trainings with Frappe. From Frappe local, our city-level event, to Frappeverse, our country-level event. 
- **Lead sharing:** Once Partners start to get recognised in the community for their quality, reliability and growth, our sales team starts to share qualified leads. The # and quality of leads shared with partners grow as partners climb up the partnership tiers.



# Customers prefer authorised partners

There are hundreds (and possibly thousands) of freelancers who resell/implement Frappe products to end customers. Choosing the wrong partner can lead to losing money, but most importantly, losing time. With authorised partners, customers get the assurance about:



- **Quality:** Frappe’s partnership program is built on the tenet of Quality. Partner Maturity Model (PMM) is a process where Frappe audits our partners to ensure they have systems and processes that ensure consistent quality services. This is done to ensure that when customers do business with Frappe partners, they get a gold standard experience. These scores are made visible on the Partner Profile, measured using an objective 5-point score.
- **Proficiency:** Frappe partners have to complete a minimum number of product certifications. These certifications are listed on their profile too. Frappe’s product certifications are obtained after a rigorous course and evaluation. It makes sure that consultants on the ground are adept with the fundamental concepts as well as understanding of all the features. 
- **OEM support:** Business software like ERPs are mission critical in the journey of customers. Working with authorised partners assures customers that the partner they’re working with has direct access and support from the OEM, which they indeed do. This way, customers can get access to OEM via partners, which is required to resolve unexpected product issues and tricky questions.



# Tips before you start the partnership

- **Get trained and certified:** Invest in learning through official documentation, courses, and certifications. A strong foundation ensures a high success rate for your first new implementations. Getting 2 team members certified also helps you understand the deep capabilities of the product, and meet the minimum eligibility criteria to become a partner. 
- **Collaborate with partners in your region:** Collaborate with experienced partners in your region. This helps you learn about their journey, challenges and growth within the Frappe partnership. Having an experienced partner works as a rock solid support for succeeding at first few implementations, and avoid common pitfalls. 
- **Implement for your own company:** Use Frappe products within your own organisation to understand real-world use cases. You can start with FrappeHR, FrappeCRM or ERPNext. Some of the best partners in our ecosystem are customer-turned-partners. Implementing Frappe products before reselling them builds deeper insight, conviction and credibility when engaging with customers, and enables you to pitch Frappe with clarity and confidence.
- **Create a few sites on Frappe Cloud:** The eligibility and journey of your partnership revolves primarily around Frappe Cloud. The Partnership makes sense if you see merit in using Frappe Cloud to host your Frappe customers. Hence, partner prospects are expected to give Frappe Cloud a try, host a couple of sites - could be for your own company or customers you’ve implemented - before taking up a partnership. 
- **Complete 1-2 implementations:** Unlike licensed business software, becoming a partner is not mandatory to resell/implement ERPNext. If you already have customers interested in the business, it is strongly suggested to get a few implementations completed before taking the partnership. This way you know what you’re getting into.
