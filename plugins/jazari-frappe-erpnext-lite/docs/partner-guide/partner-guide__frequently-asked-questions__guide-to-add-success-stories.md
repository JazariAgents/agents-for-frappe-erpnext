### **Why should partners add Success Stories?**

- Success Stories drive traffic on the partner’s profile page from the following sources
  - [https://frappe.io/stories](https://frappe.io/stories)  
  - [https://frappe.io/erpnext/industry](https://frappe.io/erpnext/industry)
- Strengthen the partner’s profile page for new prospects.
- Helps earn industry expertise. E.g., Partners with >= 2 stories in a particular industry will receive a badge that shows expertise in that industry. Similarly, partners with >=2 stories for a Frappe App will receive a badge to show expertise in implementing this app.



### **How to add Success Stories?**

- Add your stories using this form: [https://frappe.io/customer-success-story/new.](https://frappe.io/customer-success-story/new)
- Strictly follow the guidelines mentioned [here.](https://frappe.io/customer-stories-guidelines)
- Do not misquote or misrepresent facts.
- Once approved by Frappe, it will be published.
