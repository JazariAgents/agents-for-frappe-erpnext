# How to reach Silver tier

### **Additional Benefits**

- **More trust:** Silver partners are expected to meet a PMM level 4, which is more stringent and demonstrates higher process excellence compared to level 3, which is mandatory for Bronze. Achieving PMM Level 4 significantly enhances trust and credibility.
- **Better listing:** Silver partners are visible on the [main partner listing page](https://frappe.io/partners/list), right below Gold, with a large card layout. They get a profile page to list their company information, website, address, success stories, and strengths.
- **More leads:** With better listing, website mentions and recognition, the Silver Partner profile page receives higher website traffic and more leads.
- **High-value deals:** Silver partners get priority listing and attract higher-value enterprise prospects.

### **Eligibility Criteria**

- **Monthly recurring revenue of $3150 / INR 2,50,000 or above on Frappe Cloud**. This includes revenue from both directly and indirectly billed accounts. This approximately means ~12 small business customers (with ~$50 sites on Frappe Cloud) or 3-4 mid to large sized customers.
- **PMM Score of 4 or above:** You must initiate a PMM audit at least 60 days prior to your expected upgrade date. Our team of experts will audit your processes. And only after you successfully meet the PMM Score 3 criteria, you will be listed as a Silver partner.



The MRR contribution increases every year. It is recommended that you verify the criteria with the Frappe team.
