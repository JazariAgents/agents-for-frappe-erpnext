# Code of conduct

This project is for people who run businesses on Frappe and ERPNext, and for the people who help them. Many
are new to the software, and many are writing in a second language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Keep to the subject: the plugin, the site it ran on, and what happened.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Do not post anyone's private data: customer names, keys, addresses, or company figures.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination of any kind, sexual language or imagery, and publishing someone's
private information.

## If something goes wrong

Write to the maintainer at the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are kept private. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
people who keep breaking them.
